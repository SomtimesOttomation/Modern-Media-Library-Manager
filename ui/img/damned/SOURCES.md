# Souls of the Damned images

All images here were generated by the project owner with AI image tools (OpenAI and Google Gemini), whose terms let
the person who made them use them, and were then cut out, graded and reshaped for MMLM. No stock or film images.

- skull-*.png: one AI-generated bloody skull, reshaped into four head shapes, each with six variants (fangs, short
  mouth, glowing eyes and combinations).
- horned.png, clown.png: AI-generated heads (Gemini).
- hand0-3.png: AI-generated hands (Gemini).
